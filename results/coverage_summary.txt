======================================================================
CAVEATS — read before quoting the relay numbers above
======================================================================
1. The top-ranked "relay" site (30.418, 94.839) sits at 4510 m elevation — a mountaintop 2193 m
   ABOVE the gateway. From a 5000 m peak almost every valley point has line of sight, which is
   why one relay "serves 97% of the unreachable points".
   => The relay figures are an UPPER BOUND under an unrealistic siting assumption. Real relay
   sites need road/access, installability, and solar exposure. A siting-constrained version
   (elevation caps, slope limits, access proxies) would give much lower numbers and is the
   correct next step.
2. One sampled point returned 42.7 dB total loss at 3.75 km, BELOW the 48.6 dB free-space value.
   ITM's reference attenuation can go negative (LoS/super-refractive gain), so this is not
   necessarily a bug — but it should be checked before publication.
3. "Loss over reachable points: min 0.0 dB" is an artifact of co-located grid points
   (distance clamped at 0.05 km) and should be excluded from any reported range.
4. Unreachable share (27.7%) IS the robust headline: it is a single-gateway, terrain-only figure
   and does not depend on the relay assumptions.
